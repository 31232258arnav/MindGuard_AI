1. Clinical Problem & Motivation

Clinical Crisis: 1B+ individuals suffer from mental health disorders (WHO). Acute shortages of psychiatric professionals, stigma, and cost prevent 75%+ from seeking care. Suicide is the 3rd leading cause of death among youths aged 15–29.

The NLP Challenge: Early psychological distress hides in informal, unstructured digital language (journals, messaging, social posts). Binary tools (positive/negative) fail to differentiate co-morbid affective states like clinical depression and acute suicidal ideation.

4. Tri-Tier Distributed Topology ($0 Cost)
• Tier 1 (Web Edge): Single Page App hosted on GitHub Pages (100% uptime, global CDN, 0ms cold start).
• Tier 2 (GPU Tunnel): Encrypted Cloudflare edge tunnel bridges local high-power GPU PyTorch inference directly to the web.
• Tier 3 (Cloud Fallback): Containerized FastAPI ASGI backend on Render and serverless edge on Vercel.
• Tier 4 (Offline Engine): Embedded client-side JS classifier provides instant local fallback if all network drops.
5. Clinical Psychometrics (PHQ-9 & GAD-7)
PHQ-9 (Depression: 0–27): Evaluates anhedonia, fatigue, sleep disruption, appetite, and suicidal ideation under DSM-5 criteria.
GAD-7 (Anxiety: 0–21): Quantifies autonomic nervousness, panic triggers, and catastrophic worry.
Automated DSM-5 stratification delivers instant severity scoring (Minimal, Mild, Moderate, Severe) and exports clinical SOAP notes.
6. MindBot Chat & Crisis Helpline Triage
Empathetic Brain: Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash / Claude 3.5 Sonnet. Strictly bounded to mental health dialogue with evidence-based soothing (4-7-8 breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding).
