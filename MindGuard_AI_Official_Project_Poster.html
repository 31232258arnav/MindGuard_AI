1. Problem Statement & Clinical Motivation

Clinical Crisis: Over 1 billion individuals globally suffer from psychiatric conditions (WHO). Suicide ranks as the 3rd leading cause of death among youth. Shortages of clinical psychologists, stigma, and cost prevent 75%+ from seeking care.

The Linguistic Challenge: Early distress indicators manifest extensively within informal, unstructured digital text (journals, forums, chat). Conventional sentiment tools use simplistic binary labels (positive/negative), completely failing to detect complex co-morbid psychiatric states.

2. Core Research Objectives
  • 7-Class Classification: Fine-tune BERT on 7 affective categories.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Extract self-attention weights for token heatmaps.
  • Clinical Batteries: Digitize standardized DSM-5 PHQ-9 and GAD-7 tests.
  • MindBot Chat: Empathetic screener strictly bounded to mental health.
  • Crisis Escalation: Hardcoded emergency routing to Tele-MANAS (14416).
  • Zero-Cost Cloud: Resilient tri-tier topology (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Render).

4. BERT Transformer Neural Backbone
• Base Model: bert-base-uncased (12 layers, 768 hidden, 12 attention heads, 110M params).
• Input Pipeline: WordPiece tokenization (Max sequence 128 tokens) with [CLS] pooled output.
• Optimization: AdamW (ε = 1e-8), lr = 2e-5 with linear decay, 7 training epochs (~16,597 gradient steps).
• Checkpoint: Best validation accuracy at Epoch 5 (82.80%) to prevent overfitting.

6. Validated Psychometrics (PHQ-9 & GAD-7)
PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9): Standardized depression severity scoring (0–27). Evaluates anhedonia, fatigue, sleep disruption, appetite, and suicidal ideation under DSM-5 criteria.
GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7): Standardized anxiety assessment (0–21). Quantifies autonomic nervousness, catastrophic worry, and panic triggers.
Automated DSM-5 stratification delivers instant severity category assignment (Minimal, Mild, Moderate, Severe) with actionable clinical coping recommendations.
7. MindBot Neural Chat & Crisis Helpline Triage
Empathetic Brain: Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash / Claude 3.5 Sonnet. Strictly bounded to mental health dialogue with evidence-based soothing (4-7-8 breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding).

9. Explainable AI & Zero-Cost Cloud Strategy
Token Attention Saliency (XAI): Softmax attention weights from BERT's 12th layer are projected onto UI tokens. Clinicians can audit exactly which words drove affective predictions.
$0 Infrastructure Architecture: Combines GitHub Pages (static edge CDN), Cloudflare Tunnels (local GPU bridge), Render/Vercel (cloud backups), and embedded JS heuristics for 99.99% availability at $0.00/month.
