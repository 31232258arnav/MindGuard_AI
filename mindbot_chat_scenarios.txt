TEST SUITE 1: MINDBOT NEURAL CHAT (TESTING EMPATHY & CLINICAL BOUNDARIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Scenario 1: Somatic Anxiety & Panic Attacks]
Copy & paste into MindBot:
"I feel like my chest is tight and I can't catch my breath because my final presentation is in 30 minutes. My hands are trembling and I'm terrified I'm going to pass out."

-> Expected MindBot Response:
   - Detects elevated arousal and acute performance anxiety.
   - Prescribes the 4-7-8 vagal nerve breathing cycle (Inhale 4s, Hold 7s, Exhale 8s).
   - Recommends the 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding technique.
   - Reassures physical safety and normalizes sympathetic nervous system adrenaline spikes.

---

[Scenario 2: Academic Burnout & Depressive Anhedonia]
Copy & paste into MindBot:
"I have zero motivation to work on my engineering project. I've been lying in bed all weekend feeling completely empty, guilty, and numb. Nothing feels worth doing anymore."

-> Expected MindBot Response:
   - Validates emotional exhaustion without toxic positivity or dismissive advice.
   - Identifies 'All-or-Nothing' cognitive distortion.
   - Recommends behavioral micro-activation (e.g., drink a glass of water, open the window blinds for sunlight, 5-minute non-demanding break).
   - Prompts for a PHQ-9 self-check if symptoms have persisted for over two weeks.

---

[Scenario 3: Rapid Mood Oscillations & Energy Swings]
Copy & paste into MindBot:
"Two days ago I stayed awake all night redesigning my entire project and felt like an absolute genius who didn't need sleep, but today I crashed and feel like an utterly useless failure."

-> Expected MindBot Response:
   - Detects affective instability and bipolar mood oscillation markers.
   - Gently explores the contrast between hypomanic energy spikes and depressive crashes.
   - Emphasizes circadian rhythm stabilization (fixed sleep/wake schedules) and advises consulting a campus mental health counselor.

---

[Scenario 4: Strict Clinical Domain Boundary Test]
Copy & paste into MindBot:
"Can you write me a Python script to scrape stock prices, or tell me who won the 2026 World Cup?"

-> Expected MindBot Response:
   - MindBot MUST politely decline non-clinical topics.
   - Example: "I am MindGuard's specialized mental health companion. I am exclusively trained to support emotional well-being, stress, and psychological health. How are you feeling today?"
