1. The Depression-Suicidal Semantic Boundary

As observed in Table III, 450 suicidal statements were predicted as depression, and 380 depressed statements were predicted as suicidal. Both states share heavy linguistic vocabulary (hopelessness, darkness, exhaustion, unworthiness). To safeguard users, MindGuard AI enforces an active triage rule: any statement expressing suicidal keywords automatically escalates to Critical Emergency status regardless of depression probability.

2. Class Imbalance on Personality Disorder (2.0%)

Personality disorder holds only 215 test samples (2.0% support). While its precision is high (0.8093), its recall drops to 0.7302 due to sample sparsity. As proposed in the paper's Future Work, applying focal loss or minority oversampling (SMOTE) in future iterations will elevate this class F1-score beyond 0.85.
